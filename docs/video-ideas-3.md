# 17 more video ideas for the feed

A third, short list, numbered 1001 to 1017, drawn up once nearly every idea scored 80 or more in the [first](video-ideas.md) and [second](video-ideas-2.md) lists had been built. The ideas left in those two top out at 77, and the best of them sit close to a video already in the feed (Rush hour next to the parking jam, Nuts and bolts next to water sort). These are formats that are watched now and that nothing in the 433 videos covers.

## How the score works

The same grid as the [second list](video-ideas-2.md#how-the-score-works), the same bands: **T**rend strength (0 – 25), **R**eadability at 32 × 56 (0 – 20), **M**otion and looping (0 – 20), **B**udget (0 – 10), **F**it and novelty against the 433 videos now in the feed (0 – 15), Ris**k** (0 – 10, 10 is no risk). The score is the sum.
