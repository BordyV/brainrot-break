# 500 more video ideas for the feed

A second list of 500 TikTok-style video ideas for brainrot-break, numbered 501 to 1000, in 20 new themes. None repeats an idea from the [first list](video-ideas.md) or a video already in the feed.

## How the score works

The score weighs the same six criteria as the first list. Here they are written out as points, so that every idea is scored the same way and a 70 here means what a 70 meant there. The score is the sum.

| Criterion | Points | What earns them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T**rend strength | 0 – 25 | How much this kind of clip is watched on TikTok and Shorts |
| **R**eadability at 32 × 56 | 0 – 20 | It survives at a few dozen pixels, with no text and no sound |
| **M**otion and looping | 0 – 20 | It moves all the time and loops or resets by itself, rather than being a scene or a single gag |
| **B**udget | 0 – 10 | Under 1 ms a frame and under 131,072 SVG characters. Noise, dithering and gradients cost points |
| **F**it and novelty | 0 – 15 | How far it is from the 342 videos now in the feed, and how well it suits developers waiting on Claude |
| Ris**k** | 0 – 10 | 10 is no risk. Named memes, brands, characters and real people lose points, because the repository will be public |
