# 500 video ideas for the feed

A list of 500 TikTok-style video ideas for brainrot-break, in 20 themes, each scored out of 100 for how well it would work as a pixel simulation in the feed.

## How the score works

A score is a judgement, not a measurement. It weighs:

- **Trend strength:** how much this kind of clip is watched on TikTok and Shorts.
- **Readability at 32 × 56:** whether the idea survives at a few dozen pixels, with no text and no sound.
- **Motion and looping:** a simulation that moves all the time and loops or resets by itself scores higher than a scene or a single gag.
- **Budget:** under 1 ms a frame and under 131,072 SVG characters. Noise, dithering and gradients cost points.
- **Fit and novelty:** how far it is from the 28 videos already in the feed, and how well it suits developers waiting on Claude.
- **Risk:** named memes, brands, characters and real people lose points, because the repository will be public.

| Band | Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 | 85 – 100 | Build it next |
| 🟡 | 70 – 84 | Good, with care |
| 🟠 | 55 – 69 | Possible, weak at this size or off-trend |
| 🔴 | under 55 | Skip |
